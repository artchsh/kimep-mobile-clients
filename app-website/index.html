Unofficial Android client · not by KIMEP

Your timetable, without the portal.

A student-built Android app for the KIMEP student portal: schedule, grades and the academic calendar, with class reminders. It talks to the same old mobile APIs the store app used.

03 — What it does

Features

  1. 01

    Schedule

    Your weekly timetable grouped by day, with today highlighted, a midterm-week banner and pull to refresh. Cached, so it opens instantly and updates in the background.

  2. 02

    Grades

    Cumulative GPA and credits, current-term assessment scores, and your full transcript grouped by semester.

  3. 03

    Academic calendar

    The official calendar PDF parsed into the app — both academic years, every semester, with the current date range marked.

  4. 04

    Class reminders

    Notifications one hour and ten minutes before each class, plus a final-exam reminder. Each one can be switched off individually.

  5. 05

    Final exams

    A dedicated view for the exam timetable, once the university publishes it.

  6. 06

    Material You

    Native Material 3 design that follows your phone's colours, light and dark, edge to edge.

04 — What it knows about you

Privacy

The app can send anonymous usage statistics — which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped, and a random ID generated on your device — to a self-hosted Umami instance, so the developer can see what is used and what is not. This is a proof of concept.

It never collects your name, student ID, password, session, grades, courses or anything you view, and it never sends real URLs. Tracking is on by default with a clearly labelled notice on first launch and a one-tap opt-out in Settings; switching it off also forgets the random ID.

If the developer ever shares this data, it would only be aggregates with KIMEP administration to inform product decisions. Nothing is shared today.
