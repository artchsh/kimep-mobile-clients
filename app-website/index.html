KIMEP Mobile

An unofficial Android client for KIMEP students.

Schedule, grades, academic calendar and class reminders — without using the old student portal.

Download APK GitHub

Unofficial project — not affiliated with or endorsed by KIMEP University.

Features

  • ScheduleWeekly timetable grouped by day, with today highlighted and a midterm-week notice. Opens from cache and updates in the background.
  • GradesCumulative GPA and credits, current-term assessment scores, and the full transcript by semester.
  • Academic calendarThe official calendar parsed into the app, for both academic years and every semester.
  • Final examsA dedicated view for the exam timetable, once the university publishes it.
  • Class remindersNotifications one hour and ten minutes before each class, plus a final-exam reminder. Each can be switched off.
  • Material YouNative Material 3 design that follows your phone's colours, light and dark.

Privacy

The app can send anonymous usage statistics — which screens are opened, which buttons are tapped, and a random ID generated on your device — so the developer can see what is used and what is not. It never collects your name, student ID, password, session, grades, courses or anything you view, and never sends real URLs.

Tracking is on by default, with a notice on first launch and a one-tap opt-out in Settings. Switching it off also forgets the random ID. Nothing is shared today; in the future it may be shared as aggregates with KIMEP administration.
